- Fixed a user turn staying open after a mute ended, such as `FunctionCallUserMuteStrategy` muting while the user was still speaking. A mute in `LLMUserAggregator` now applies from the next user turn: the turn already open still receives its speech stops and transcripts, while audio, speech starts and interruptions stay muted. A speech stop whose start passed unmuted also gets through a mute before any turn has opened.
